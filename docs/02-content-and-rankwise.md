# Content Quality & 49-Factor Matrix

This document outlines the content evaluation procedures based on the RankWise standard.

## 1. Keyword Optimization & Density
- **Density Target**: Aim for 0.8% - 1.5%. Lower than 0.8% is under-optimized; higher than 3% is keyword stuffing.
- **Placements**: The primary keyword MUST appear in:
  - The `<h1>` (Title)
  - The first 100-150 words of the body
  - At least one `<h2>` subheading
  - Meta Description
  - URL Slug (if possible)
- **LSI / Semantic Keywords**: Include 5-15 related terms naturally throughout the content to build topical relevance.

## 2. Readability & Phrasing
- **Readability Target**: Grade 7-9 for English. Adjust for other languages.
- **Passive Voice**: Should be ≤10% of sentences. Prefer active voice.
- **Transition Words**: Aim for ≥30% of sentences (e.g., "however," "therefore," "in addition") to improve flow.
- **Paragraph Length**: Keep paragraphs under 3 sentences or ~150 words. Ensure varied sentence lengths to create rhythm.

## 3. Title & Heading Structure
- **Heading Hierarchy**: Enforce strict `H1 -> H2 -> H3` nesting. No skipping levels.
- **Title Formula**: Use the formula `[Number] + [Power Word] + [Keyword] + [Curiosity Gap/Benefit]`.
- **Power Words**: Use at least 2 emotionally charged words (e.g., "Proven," "Secret," "Ultimate").
- **Sentiment**: Ensure the title has clear positive or negative sentiment; neutral titles have lower CTR.

## 4. Internal & External Linking
- **Internal Links**: Include 3-10 internal links. Use varied anchors: partial match (30-40%), branded (20-25%), generic (20-25%), exact match (≤2).
- **External Links**: Include 2-5 links to highly authoritative sources (.edu, .gov, known brands). Ensure at least one is DoFollow.
